MoodswingsFan Hardware & Circuit Architecture

ESP32 Microcontroller ➔ IRF520 MOSFET Gate ➔ 12V 4-Pin PWM DC Motor

The MoodswingsFan physical system pairs computer vision with a high-torque 12V DC brushless ventilation fan. Speed transitions are mediated via 25 kHz PWM signals to ensure whisper-quiet operation without acoustic motor hum.
